## Why the fallback still verifies signatures

`BlsEnvelope::context` returns only the application-context bytes. `BlsEnvelope::verify` (`radicle-crypto/src/repository.rs:460–480`) compares the whole reconstructed subject and expected authority, then verifies BLS over the exact framed message. Identity binds actual document blob/RID; COB binds actual tree and unsigned metadata; signed refs bind actual refs, parent and repository (`storage/refs/sigrefs/read.rs:86–117`). Thus an ordinary reader accepts signed nonzero contexts without treating those bytes as a substitute for cryptographic verification. Managed authorization remains a separate, stricter input.
