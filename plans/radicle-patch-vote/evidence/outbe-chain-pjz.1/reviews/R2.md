## R2-F1 — blocking liveness defect: distinct valid manifests permanently split one sequence

Location: `design.md:164–177,196–199,252–256`; model `p0-check/src/publication.rs:70–87`.

The manifest commits q distinct current partial frames for each native subject. Sorting frames by index does not make the selected q-subset unique. Two correct distributed collectors can recover the **same** unique native BLS signature from `{0,1,2}` and `{1,2,3}`, yet hash different manifests. Every current participant is restricted to one body per sequence. There is no specified candidate selection, attempt/round, unlock, expiry or retry transition for an incomplete split. A stable committee does not resolve it.

Concrete n=4/q=3 execution, with no Byzantine participant:

1. Collector A prepares valid manifest A and collector B prepares valid manifest B for the same approved job/predecessor, differing only in contribution subset.
2. Participants 0 and 1 first endorse A; participants 2 and 3 first endorse B. All four attestations arrive on chain.
3. Each body has two attestations, so neither creates a receipt. Every slot for this sequence is occupied.
4. Even after all parties receive both manifests and want the same candidate, the one-body-per-sequence rule rejects every further endorsement. Sequence cannot advance. A later committee transition could clear it, but the stated eventual-stable-committee liveness assumption permits no later transition.

The body also contains a finalized anchor selected from checked local finality, without requiring a unique job-derived anchor (`design.md:196–199`); different honest latest finalized views are another source of competing body hashes.

Independent reproduction extends an isolated copy of the existing reduced model with two manifest hashes, signs with four genuine current MinPk keys, then asserts no receipt and rejection of all convergence retries. Full extended model is `R2-split-model.rs` (SHA256 `3eaab2dc91bfe1555a20daa00eb0e5ebc73442892f4835802109abd0c835f73d`). Output in `R2-checks.log`:

```text
REPRODUCED R2-F1: all 4 honest current participants delivered, 2+2 split; no checkpoint and all convergence retries rejected
```

Required correction: specify a safe bounded mechanism that makes honest publication bodies converge or lets an unfinished split recover, including contribution proofs, anchor choice and competing collectors. Preserve distributed collection without a designated proposer and current-quorum safety. Add a reduced-model scenario with competing valid candidates, staggered honest delivery and eventual convergence under a stable committee. This is required P0 design evidence; no production handler is needed to fix this finding.
