## Descriptor review and independent reconstruction

`design.md` §5 now gives an explicit OGD1 binary grammar with byte lengths and limits. Its fields are schema, chain_id, genesis timestamp, fixed name/branch, description, initial Git tree, exact pre-genesis content-manifest hash, policy and key source. No field depends on the actual genesis block, DKG key, RID or descriptor hash. Seeding OGD1 and its SHA-256 therefore does not require a fixed point.

After genesis/DKG, OPB1 includes the actual genesis hash and seeded descriptor hash together with owner/RID/material/manifest commitments. Its verifier must match both the seeded descriptor and its own chain identity. The same descriptor across two different genesis hashes is not a publication-authority replay. This is a concrete serialization/dependency argument; the fixture does not pretend to run a genesis builder or DKG ceremony.
