## Prior R2 findings and new-contract checks

**R2-F1: resolved.** `design.md` §4 removes public DKG proof and q partial frames from the committed manifest. They are separately validated interchangeable witnesses; they cannot alter native objects, refs, envelope or stable manifest. The job has one anchor: genesis for bootstrap, epoch activation for BindMaterial, and maximum-height terminal approval/material-binding/previous-receipt block for Review/Merge. Local newest finalized tip is not used. §6 pins graph slice, parents/related/resource, actor/time/message, action JSON and complete owner refs to immutable approved/accepted inputs, not collector-local COB tips. Thus honest validators independently derive one body. With at most f Byzantine participants, at least n−f=q honest slots converge; no unlock or designated collector/proposer is required.

Reproduction uses real shares: main.rs verifies both subsets 012 and 123 against the same polynomial and identical recovered native signatures (including every RDB1 subject). The model supplies different witnesses/local finalized tips to the pinned candidate and reproduces the former staggered schedule, reaching one checkpoint with three attestations and without the fourth participant. Its omission of full production body/job assembly is explicit. The omitted preparation details now have a concrete source-compatible contract; real two-collector/native-package production tests remain mandatory in P6/P8.

**R2-F2: resolved.** The normative metadata input is the exact unsigned Git commit content, excluding only one physical BLS signature header; field order, parents, numeric/time encoding, delimiters/LF, message/trailers and forbidden headers/CR/NUL/folding are specified. ## Crypto and threat-model assessment

- The signature construction is actual Commonware d476a236 MinSig, G2 public key 96 bytes/G1 signature 48 bytes, with the specified POP ciphersuite hash-to-G1 DST. Threshold sign/verify/recover source, decoder infinity/subgroup checks and `union_unique` length-prefix namespace framing were checked live. Native BLS is not replaced by individual identity signatures.
- DKG material reuse is a narrow fixed-domain typed-job interface, not a share export or public arbitrary-message oracle. Repository, partial-frame and publication namespaces are disjoint from seed/consensus namespaces (`proof/constants.rs:206–229`; Commonware `utils/src/lib.rs:161–175`). Under stated BLS/hash-to-curve assumptions, repository signatures do not supply seed-domain signatures or new secret information; a passing cross-domain vector is feasibility evidence, not a standalone proof of stronger VRF security. Existing DKG/VRF secrecy assumptions remain necessary.
- Fresh group-key reset under the same chain identity is explicitly outside the normal resharing lifecycle and fails closed; membership/polynomial/generation changes retain native identity/RID. Old shares are not claimed cryptographically revoked. Their new-write power is constrained by current MinPk identities plus finalized receipts and the receiver's trusted freshness.
- Current effective consensus participants differ from lifecycle ACTIVE, including retained accountability during exits/jail. The patch-only cast/tally hook selects ACTIVE∩effective; other targets keep legacy behavior. Current signers may execute Approved even after voting No. This preserves the user constraint without rewriting generic Vote semantics.
- An ordinary author can publish/revise/fetch a patch before Vote. Owner entry receipt checks follow reachable entries across namespaces and cache/direct lookup/reopen/rebuild, preventing an author ref from laundering a valid old-owner forgery. Pure author updates do not require owner checkpoints. This is an explicit implementable admission predicate, not a claim of production coverage today.
- Unknown/untrusted managed policy or peer/RPC-declared key/set fails closed. New writes require exact current receipt and local CAS ticket. Earlier accepted receipt permits only exact immutable history; latest canonical promotion cannot roll back. Missing dependencies wait; receipt-bound recovery replays exact bytes.
- Consensus processing remains bounded to one identity attestation and ≤256 slots per block, with shared artifact budget/reservations. Git/network waits and threshold collection are outside block execution. Stable committee, q available shares/attesters, pinned-object availability and eventual delivery remain necessary for liveness. Continuous churn/partition or compromised current quorum/local validating process is not covered by the guarantee.
- Exact patch/revision is approved, then native Review Accept and Merge use separate checkpoints. TargetMoved/conflict remains approved-but-unmerged, no automatic new edits/rebase/squash, and recovery/journal/announcement order is fixed. Native Merge branch/ancestry behavior is source-grounded in `radicle/src/cob/patch.rs:1247–1310`.
