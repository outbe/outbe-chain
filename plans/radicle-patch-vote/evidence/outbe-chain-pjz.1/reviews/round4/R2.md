## Batch, cadence and liveness assessment

The new record is precisely `OPB1[477] || count:u8 || count*(index:u32be || MinPkSignature[96])`, with strictly increasing indices and count1..32. Maximum payload `477+1+32*100=3678`; record overhead tag/u16 length adds3, giving3681. It remains independent individual current-identity endorsements in addition to native threshold BLS. The design validates the entire batch before effects, rejects malformed/duplicate/bad last entries atomically, records valid slots and tallies once, including a threshold reached inside a batch. This is concretely implementable with existing checkpoint/rollback storage patterns; actual P3 batch parser/runtime tests are still required.

Source `crates/blockchain/primitives/src/reshare_artifact.rs:55–92,200–260` fixes current record overhead/caps: summary32, timestamp8, CE-root36, late-finalize K3 and bitmap≤32. `reshare_artifact/late_finalize.rs:8–58` encodes each maximum credit as96 fixed bytes+2 length+32 bitmap+96 aggregate signature. Thus maximum current **non-DKG** artifact envelope plus batch is:

```text
6 + (3+32) + (3+8) + (3+36) + [3+2+3*(96+2+32+96)] + 3681
= 4455 bytes < 65536
```

This independently checked source arithmetic supports P0 feasibility, and does not claim a production assembly test or measured gas. Activation/boundary blocks may be large; the design excludes them from required usable capacity and preserves all mandatory reservations. Outside the pre-freeze window, collector/proposer accepts a maximal fitting prefix or waits, never discarding mandatory artifacts.

Actual cadence source `engine/src/stack/dkg/handoff.rs:10–38` clamps prepare to epoch length and defines freeze as last activation+length−effective prepare. `epoch/ceremony/freeze.rs:24–77` launches a ceremony regardless of whether participants changed; `dkg/persistence.rs:240–274` and `epoch/activation.rs:113–138` create the next epoch/material. Stable membership therefore does not mean stable material. Genesis inputs expose both timings (`scripts/genesis_stages.py:68–77`). The new feature-enabled guard `length−min(prepare,length)≥64` is aligned with these live semantics, while feature-disabled existing timings stay compatible.

At maxn256, q171 and cap32 require6 available batch slots per checkpoint; BindMaterial→Review→Merge needs18 rather than513 individual slots. Same-set resharing continues and current material checks stay strict. FIFO resumes oldest approval after binding, preserves a prior finalized Review instead of restarting it, and retries incomplete current-generation work safely. Window miss retains approval as WaitingForWindow and does not delay consensus or extend stale signing authority.

The finite model checks five periodic materials at default300/30,300/200,1200/30 and minimum accepted65/1 timings, with maxn and n4, bounded preparation/finality/delivery waits and omitted/busy blocks. Maxn checkpoints complete at blocks13/26/38; unsupported short windows are rejected. Its repeated generations are a **capacity/scenario calculation**, not a full DKG or real asynchronous sidecar simulation. The contract expressly conditions progress on q responsive sidecars, pinned object availability, timely sequential readiness/finality/delivery, adequate budget and fair inclusion providing required slots. It makes no progress claim for arbitrary delays, perpetual budget starvation or unbounded churn. Those assumptions must be measured/proven in P3/P6/P8; they are not production facts established here.
