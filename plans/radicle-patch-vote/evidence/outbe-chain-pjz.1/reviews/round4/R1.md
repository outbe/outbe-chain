## Capacity and atomicity assessment

The fixed batch grammar is internally consistent: `477 + 1 + 32*(4+96) = 3678` payload bytes; the existing tag/u16-length record header gives **3681**. There is one body/context check, at most32 individual MinPk verifications independent of n/q, strictly increasing indices, all-entry validation before effects and one final tally. This avoids a mid-batch receipt changing sequence before later entries are checked. Bad-last-entry rollback and q-crossing are explicit future production tests, not claimed model results.

At n256, q=171; `ceil(171/32)=6`, so BindMaterial→Review→Merge needs18 full available batch slots rather than513 single-attestation slots. Current source shows why stable membership alone is insufficient: material rotates anyway. The selected feature-enabled guard `epochLength−min(prepare,epochLength)≥64` creates a pre-freeze interval without new next-ceremony dealer/preannounce traffic after the activation block. The finite schedule deliberately omits early/artifact-busy blocks and every third block, adds finality/preparation waits, and finishes n256 stages at **[13,26,38]**, before freeze for all tested cadences including65/1.

Independently checked all n=1…256 at the shortest accepted65/1 cadence and tested guard equivalence with actual prepare clamping for every length0…400/prepare0…401. No count/window mismatch was found.

An independent static maximum non-DKG wire sum from live codec widths is **4455 bytes**: batch3681 plus774 for OART envelope6, summary35, timestamp11, CE root39 and LFC record683 (`3+2+3*(96+2+32+96)`). This supports P0 feasibility under the stated no-new-DKG-window condition; it is not an actual future codec/assembly or gas test. P3 explicitly must prove that integration with mandatory records, and P6/P8 must prove real timely readiness and delivery.

Liveness is correctly conditional. FIFO resumes oldest approved work after binding, or Merge if Review was already checkpointed. Old pending signatures do not transfer generations. A missed window preserves approval and a visible wait, while consensus/epoch progress continues. The contract does not promise completion with arbitrary fetch delay, permanent artifact congestion, insufficient responsive participants or unfair inclusion.
